## Start here

This handbook collects the course information you will return to throughout the term. Begin by reading the assignment briefs, add the teaching sessions to your calendar, and use Canvas for announcements, submissions and grades.

## How to use this handbook

- **Schedule** shows teaching sessions, rooms and deadlines.
- **Assignments** explains what to make and how the work is assessed.
- **Resources** collects course guides, slides, templates and useful references.
- **Support** tells you where to ask questions and book feedback.
