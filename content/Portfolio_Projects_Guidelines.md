# **Portfolio projects**

## **Overview**

Throughout this course, you will build a professional portfolio by completing a series of creative projects.

Your portfolio should showcase your skills, creative thinking and ability to communicate ideas through design. By the end of the course, it should reflect the type of work you want to be recognised for as a creative while demonstrating a consistent level of quality across all projects.

## **Portfolio structure**

Your final portfolio should include:

* A consistent portfolio identity.  
* One required Motion project.  
* Two portfolio briefs of your choice.  
* Any additional projects you wish to include.

You are encouraged to add additional projects if they strengthen your portfolio.

## **Developing your portfolio**

Every project should strengthen your portfolio and demonstrate your skills as a designer.

As you develop each project, think about how it contributes to your portfolio as a whole. Aim to create projects that are visually engaging, professionally presented and representative of the type of work you want to be known for.

Depending on your concept, your projects may include relevant examples of:

* Branding  
* Visual identity  
* Motion  
* Website design  
* UI design  
* Packaging  
* Photography  
* Art direction  
* Social media content  
* Campaign concepts  
* Editorial design  
* Digital products  
* Printed material  
* High-quality mockups

Choose the deliverables that best communicate your concept and present your work in a professional way.

## **Portfolio platforms**

Choose the platform that best supports the type of portfolio you want to build.

Examples include:

* Framer  
* Webflow  
* Adobe Portfolio  
* Behance  
* Cargo  
* Wix  
* Squarespace  
* Figma (Prototype)  
* A custom-coded website

Your portfolio should:

* Be publicly accessible.  
* Be easy to navigate.  
* Work well on desktop and mobile devices.  
* Present each project as a complete and engaging case study.

## **Portfolio identity**

Before developing your portfolio projects, create a visual identity for your portfolio.

Your identity should communicate your personality, creative direction and the type of work you want to be recognised for. It should create a consistent experience across your entire portfolio and be reflected throughout every project.

Your portfolio identity may include:

* Logo or wordmark  
* Typography  
* Colour palette  
* Graphic elements  
* Motion  
* Photography style  
* Layout principles

Your identity should support your work rather than compete with it, creating a cohesive visual experience across your portfolio.

## **Required project**

### **Motion**

Motion is an important part of modern digital communication and is used across branding, social media, advertising, websites and digital products.

Develop a motion project for a brand, product, service or concept of your choice. Use animation, typography, video and sound to communicate your idea in a clear, engaging and visually compelling way.
