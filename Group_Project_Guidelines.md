# Project

# **Launching a digital-first brand**

## **Overview**

In this project, you may choose to work individually or in teams of up to three students.

Your task is to develop and launch a complete digital-first brand based on one of the fictional scenarios provided.

You will develop a complete brand experience by combining branding, storytelling, content creation, motion design and digital communication into one cohesive concept.

The project simulates a real client where your team is responsible for taking a brand from concept to launch. Your final presentation should demonstrate strategic thinking, creative execution and professional presentation skills suitable for a design portfolio.

## **Teamwork**

You may complete this project individually or in teams of up to three students.

If working in a team, all members are expected to contribute actively throughout the project. Tasks may be divided based on individual strengths, but the final outcome should reflect a shared creative vision, a shared effort of work load and a consistent level of quality.

## **Learning objectives**

* Develop a strong brand concept.  
* Create a cohesive visual identity for digital media.  
* Apply storytelling throughout a complete brand experience.  
* Produce engaging content for digital platforms.  
* Use motion and sound to strengthen communication.  
* Design for multiple digital touchpoints.  
* Present and justify design decisions professionally.  
* Collaborate effectively within a creative team.

## **Deliverable 1 – Concept pitch presentation**

Deadline: Week 37

Submit a **Concept Pitch** that introduces your initial idea for the project.

This is a **hand-in**, not a presentation. The purpose is to communicate your initial direction and establish a clear creative foundation before moving into production.

Your submission should consist of **a maximum of five (5) slides** that clearly present your concept. At this stage, the focus should be on communicating your vision rather than showcasing finished designs.

### **Suggested content**

* Brand name  
* Problem or opportunity  
* Target audience  
* Brand positioning  
* Moodboard  
* Creative direction  
* Visual references  
* Initial content ideas

The Concept Pitch should establish a clear creative direction and provide a strong foundation for the remainder of the project.

## **Deliverable 2 – Final presentation**

Submit one professional presentation created in Figma Slides.

There are no minimum or maximum requirements regarding the number of slides. Include the amount of content needed to communicate your concept clearly and convincingly.

Focus on presenting the finished brand experience rather than documenting the entire design process.

The audience should understand the company, target audience, positioning, visual identity, communication, customer experience and how the brand comes to life across relevant touchpoints.

## **Project guidelines**

Every project is different, and there is no single correct solution.

Your final brand should communicate a complete, cohesive and believable brand experience. The exact deliverables are entirely up to your team and should be determined by what best supports your concept.

Depending on your chosen brief, your project may include relevant examples of:

* Brand strategy and positioning  
* Logo and visual identity  
* Colour palette and typography  
* Brand guidelines and visual language  
* Marketing and communication strategy  
* Social media and digital content  
* Motion design and branded motion elements  
* Website or app concepts  
* Packaging design  
* Product photography and art direction  
* Campaign concepts  
* Advertising  
* Printed materials  
* Environmental graphics  
* Merchandise  
* Digital and physical brand applications  
* High-quality mockups demonstrating the brand in context

Not every project will require the same deliverables. You are encouraged to choose the elements that best communicate your concept and create a complete, consistent and memorable brand experience.

## **Presentation quality**

Your presentation should be visually engaging, well structured and easy to follow. Every slide should contribute to telling the story of the brand and provide a complete understanding of the final outcome.

## **Project briefs**

Choose **one** of the following fictional scenarios.

Each scenario represents a different client, industry and design challenge, but they all follow the same project expectations.

The scenarios are intentionally open-ended to encourage creative thinking and allow every team to develop a unique solution. You are encouraged to interpret the brief creatively and define the company's products, services, target audience, positioning and visual direction. There is no single correct solution. Your objective is to develop a complete, believable and cohesive digital-first brand experience.

Depending on your concept, you are free to decide which deliverables best communicate your idea. Your final presentation should demonstrate a complete brand experience across relevant digital and physical touchpoints.

### **Boutique Hotel or Hospitality Brand**

A boutique hotel, resort, hostel or hospitality concept is preparing for launch and needs a distinctive brand identity and digital presence that creates a memorable guest experience before, during and after every visit.

Develop a complete digital-first brand that communicates the company's vision and creates a memorable experience before, during and after a guest's stay.

### **Sustainable consumer brand**

A new company is launching a consumer product with sustainability at its core. The founders want to build a modern brand that communicates innovation, transparency and long-term value while avoiding common sustainability clichés.

Create a distinctive brand that builds trust, communicates a clear purpose and stands out in a competitive market.

### **Everyday product** 

Choose a common everyday product and completely redefine how it is perceived through branding, storytelling and design.

Transform something familiar into a desirable and memorable brand experience through creative positioning and communication.

### **Outdoor lifestyle brand**

A new Scandinavian outdoor brand wants to redefine modern outdoor living by combining functionality, sustainability and contemporary design. It can be a product of your choice, it can focus on backpacks, jackets, shoes, pants, thermos, or something completely different connected to outdoor life. 

Develop a brand experience that inspires adventure, community and a modern outdoor lifestyle across relevant customer touchpoints.

### **Digital product or startup**

A startup has developed an innovative digital product or service but lacks a strong brand identity and digital presence.

Create a digital-first brand that communicates trust, innovation and usability while creating a seamless customer experience.

### **Premium skincare brand**

A new premium skincare brand is preparing to launch in a highly competitive market. The founders have developed a high-quality product line but need a distinctive brand that communicates quality, trust and a modern lifestyle.

Develop a complete brand experience that differentiates the company through branding, storytelling and customer experience across digital and physical touchpoints.
