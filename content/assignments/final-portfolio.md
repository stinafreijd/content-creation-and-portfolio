## Overview

Your portfolio should be an edited record of what you made, how you think and the kind of creative work you want to do more of. Aim for a consistent level of quality and a clear personal direction across every case.

## Required structure

- A consistent portfolio identity: wordmark, typography, colour palette and visual language.
- One required motion project using animation, typography, video and/or sound.
- Two projects from the Creative Brief Library.
- Any additional work that strengthens the portfolio.

## Platforms

Choose the platform that best supports your work: Framer, Webflow, Adobe Portfolio, Behance, Cargo, Wix, Squarespace, Figma Prototype or a custom website are all suitable.

Your portfolio must be publicly accessible, easy to navigate on desktop and mobile, and present each project as a complete case study.

## Submission

Submit a public portfolio link through Canvas. In the Canvas comment, list the projects included in the portfolio.

## Assessment

Your portfolio is assessed as a whole: creative thinking and originality, visual communication and design quality, technical execution, professional presentation, cohesion and craftsmanship.
