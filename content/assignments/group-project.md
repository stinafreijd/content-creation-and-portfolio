## Overview

Develop and launch a complete digital-first brand based on one fictional scenario. Combine branding, storytelling, content creation, motion design and digital communication into one coherent concept.

You may work independently or in a team of up to three. If you work as a team, each member is expected to contribute actively and the final outcome should communicate a shared creative vision.

## Deliverable 1 — Concept Pitch

**Deadline: Week 37.** Submit a hand-in of no more than five slides through Canvas. It is not an oral presentation; its purpose is to communicate your initial direction before production.

Include the brand name, problem or opportunity, target audience, positioning, moodboard, creative direction, visual references and initial content ideas.

## Deliverable 2 — Final presentation

Submit one professional Figma Slides presentation through Canvas. Show the finished brand experience: the company, target audience, positioning, visual identity, communication, customer experience and relevant touchpoints.

## What to develop

Choose the deliverables that best support your idea. These might include a visual identity, campaign, content system, branded motion, website or app concept, packaging, art direction, social media, environmental graphics or high-quality mockups.

## Choose a scenario

- Boutique hotel or hospitality brand
- Sustainable consumer brand
- Everyday product
- Outdoor lifestyle brand
- Digital product or startup
- Premium skincare brand

## Assessment

Assessment focuses on strategic thinking, creativity, visual communication, design quality, cohesion and professional presentation. The submitted work is graded 3, 4 or 5.
